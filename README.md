# Atlas of Type

A 3D globe of the world's typefaces and writing systems, each in the place it was made.

**Live site:** https://lare0012.github.io/atlas-of-type/

- Spin the globe and open a country to see its typefaces, their stories, sources and images.
- Switch between typefaces and writing systems, and play the timeline.
- Open **Anatomy** on any typeface to take its letters apart: every part is measured from the font itself, with sources for each term. Zoom in and drag to look closely.
- Save favourites (kept in your browser) and download a share card for any country.
- Suggest a typeface: the form opens a ready-to-send GitHub issue on this repository. Every suggestion is checked against its source before it is added.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole site in one page.
- `img/`: the images shown in the country panels. Each one is credited in the site with its author, licence and source (public domain or Creative Commons), or is a specimen set in an open-licence (OFL) font.
- `og.png`, `favicon.svg`: the link preview image and the icon.

Fonts load from Google Fonts. The globe uses three.js r128 from cdnjs.
